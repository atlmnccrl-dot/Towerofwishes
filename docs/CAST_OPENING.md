# Who is on screen: from the title screen to Kael's tavern

In order of first appearance. Covers the title screen, the prologue (TW-001–034) and Chapter One up to Kael leaving the tavern (TW-035–043).

**Status** says what exists in the build today:
- **sprite**: real art;
- **drawn**: drawn in code;
- **none**: not in the game yet.

**Design** says whether the character needs a unique look or a reusable generic body.

## Totals for art up to the tavern

**Unique characters: 5**
- Eiryn (plus an outfit question);
- Alric;
- Alric's beast form;
- the climber;
- Kael.

**Generic bodies**
- **Townsfolk (Erdra, before the Reset):** about 4 adults plus 1 child, in colourways. Clothes would look older or different from Ashgate's 150 years later.
- **Ashgate tavern patrons and townsfolk:** about 4 adults in colourways, plus the spearwoman if kept.
- **Climber's underlings:** 1 design.

**What each generic body needs**
- Movement: idle, walk, run.
- Seated: sitting, drinking, eating.
- Gestures: point, look up.
- Reactions: panic, falling.

Only the front and front-diagonal views are needed, because they appear mostly in cutscenes.
