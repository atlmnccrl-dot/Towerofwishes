## What the game itself keeps

- **Your progress** (levels, skills, gear, souls, settings) is saved **only on your device**. We do not run servers,
  you do not make an account, and we never receive your save.
- **Purchases you make** are recorded on your device so the game knows what you own, and by Apple or Google, who
  handle the payment. We never see your card or payment details.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from **Google AdMob**: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example "Rise again"
or "Double rewards") and occasional full-screen ads between fights. Buying **No Ads** removes the ads between fights.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as:
- your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iOS),
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, language),
- how you interact with ads.

Google uses this information as described in its policies: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
and <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

**Your choices**
- **In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland**, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's
  consent form. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.
- **On iPhone and iPad**, the game asks your permission to track you across other companies' apps (App Tracking
  Transparency). If you decline, you still get ads, but not personalised ones.
- **On Android**, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Google → Ads).
- Buying **No Ads** stops the ads shown between fights.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by **Apple** (App Store) or **Google** (Google Play) under their terms and privacy
policies. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase happened, which the game uses to unlock what you bought.
**Restore purchases** (Settings or the Shop) asks your store for this confirmation again.
