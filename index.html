How to play
  • WatchEvery fighter thinks for itself: they read wind-ups, parry, dodge, or fall for a feint.
  • All OutA few times a match. The whole team attacks flat out, then is drained and can’t dodge or parry until they recover.
  • FollowTap a fighter to put the camera on them. Withdraw leaves the team injured; the fight waits.
  • DefeatLosing costs nothing. Try again, or leave to change gear, find loot or train, and come back.
