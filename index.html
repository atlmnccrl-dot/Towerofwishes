Tower of Wishes

Prologue · The Last Ascent

Twenty-two years after someone reached the top of the Tower and the world burned, a new climber sets out. You choose who. You watch. They fight.

  • WatchEvery fighter thinks for itself: they read wind-ups, parry, dodge, or fall for a feint.
  • All OutA few times a match. The whole team attacks flat out, then is drained and can’t dodge or parry until they recover.
  • ChooseYour choices change who fights beside you and how each battle starts.
Hearts

A story match costs one heart only if your whole team falls. One heart refills every 8 minutes.
