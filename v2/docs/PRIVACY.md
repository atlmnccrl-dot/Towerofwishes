# Vael: Sellsword Company — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-26. Draft for the author to review before publishing; replace the contact line._

Vael is a single-player game. It does not ask for an account, and it does not collect personal
information.

## What stays on your device

- **Your progress** (your company, gear, settings) is saved on your device only. It is not sent to us
  or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. The Settings screen can copy your progress as a
  text code so you can move it yourself.

## Purchases

- Gem packs are sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play. The store handles the payment; we never
  see your card or account details. The app receives only a confirmation that a pack was bought, to
  add the gems, and keeps a record of it on your device so a purchase is not counted twice.

## What we do not do

- No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
- No analytics or crash reporting services are built into the game.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos are used.
- No data is sold or shared.

## Children

The game has no chat, no accounts and no data collection, and can be played by all ages.
Purchases are protected by the store's own parental controls.
