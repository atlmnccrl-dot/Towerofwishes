Bannerborn — Support

Something not working, or a purchase that did not arrive? Write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

It helps to include the report from the game: Settings → Report for support → Copy. It holds the game’s version, your device model and the last errors, and nothing personal.

A purchase did not arrive. Open Settings → Restore purchases first. Things bought for good come back; a crystal pack is given when the store confirms it, which can take a few minutes.

Moving to a new phone. Settings → Back up or move save gives a code that carries your progress.

Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play.
