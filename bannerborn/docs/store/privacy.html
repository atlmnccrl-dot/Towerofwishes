Bannerborn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Bannerborn (“the game”) is published by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO] (“we”). This page says what information the game handles, who else receives any, and what you can do about it. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and asks for no name, email address, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We do not receive it.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google receives device and ad information to do that, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card or payment details.

What stays on your device

Your save (heroes, progress, settings, what you have bought, how many optional ads you watched today) is stored on your device by the game. It is included in your device’s own backup if you have that switched on. Deleting the game deletes it. The game lets you copy your save as a code to move it to another device; where that code goes is up to you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads between some battles and offers optional ads you can choose to watch for a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To choose, deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google’s software in the game collects and processes:

InformationWhat it is used for
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA where you allow tracking)Showing and limiting ads, measuring them, personalised ads where permitted
IP address, from which an approximate location (country, region, city) is derivedChoosing ads for your region, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version, network type), diagnostics and ad interactions (ads shown, tapped, watched)Delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, fixing faults

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google acts under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any personalised ad is requested, and you can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings → Ad choices. On Apple devices the game asks permission before using your device’s advertising identifier; if you say no, ads are not based on that identifier. On any device you can reset or delete the advertising identifier or turn off ad personalisation in the device’s settings. Buying Quiet March in the game removes the ads shown between battles.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play, under their terms and privacy policies. They tell the game only that a purchase succeeded, so it can give you what you bought and restore it later. We do not receive your name, card or address.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. [If you choose a store age rating or audience that includes children, the ad settings and this section must change: see docs/RELEASE.md.]

What we do not do

The game does not run analytics of its own, does not sell personal information, and does not share your save with anyone.

Keeping and deleting

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored. For information held by Google, Apple or your app store, use their own tools.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to see, correct, delete or object to the use of information about you. Because we hold none ourselves, requests about advertising data go to Google; you can still write to us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will help where we can.

Changes

If the game starts handling information differently we will update this page and its date before the change reaches you.
