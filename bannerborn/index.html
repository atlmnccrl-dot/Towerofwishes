Development

Temporary, for building the game. Watch any moment without playing to it: live tiles play the real game with a full-strength team in a sandbox (nothing is saved, your progress is untouched); the rest are cutscenes and summonings. Leaving a battle brings you back here.

  

  

  





  
How to play

  

    
Battles. Your heroes fight on their own. Each one charges an ultimate by fighting; tap the glowing card to use it, or turn on AUTO. A mark on the ground is a blow about to land: heroes step out of it when they can.

    
Orders. A fight is not won by watching. Now and then one of the dead stops, glows and winds up a heavy blow: left alone it takes a third of a hero's health. Tap that foe to break the blow off (tap any foe to call it out: your heroes turn on it and it takes more harm). Shield wall makes everyone take far less for a moment, and a heavy blow that lands on it staggers the one who threw it. Rally mends everyone and charges their ultimates. Orders cost command (the gold bars), which fills with time and with kills. AUTO casts ultimates; it does not give orders.

    
The team. You start with one place in the team. Another opens at 20%, 40%, 60% and 80% of the campaign, up to five. You can own many heroes; who marches is your choice. Drag them on the Team field: right of the line they fight in front, left of it they stand behind. Tanks in front, archers, mages and healers behind.

    
Bonds and classes. Certain mixes of callings give the whole team a bonus (shown on the Team screen). A team with a tank, a healer and damage lasts longest.

    
What the dead build. From the second land on, the horde raises works where it holds ground, and all must fall before a field is won. A watchtower shoots fast. A mortar marks where its shell will land. An obelisk mends the dead near it and makes them hit harder: break it early. A brazier spits the land's own harm under your heroes: fire, frost that slows, poison that lingers, blight that drains. A cage keeps letting more out until it is broken.

    
Growing stronger. Heroes level up by fighting. Each star raises the level cap by ten: ascend with sigils on the hero's page. Each level gives a skill point for the hero's skill tree.

    
Heroes. Everyone you have stands at the muster. Tap one to call them forward, tap again for their page: Growth, Gear (tap a piece to see what else would fit and what it changes) and Skills.

    
Gear. Stages and chests drop it; the smith sells it. Enchanted weapons burn, slow, chain, poison or heal with every blow. Spare pieces sell at the quartermaster's.

    
The market. Tap a trader to walk over. The Smith has chests and masterwork arms. The Quartermaster keeps the War Chest (gold makes every hero a little stronger, rank by rank) and buys spare gear. The Gem-Trader gives the day's free crystals. The Seer sells sigils and leads to the gates.

    
Recruiting. Crystals call new heroes through the gates. Each chapter you clear opens a rarer gate. A gate never gives you a hero you already have, and every hero still behind it is as likely as any other.

    
The muster roll. Answer it at the camp once a day: seven days of small gifts, then it starts again. Missing a day costs nothing.

    
Ads and the Treasurer. Some bonuses can be had for watching a short ad: double the camp's spoils or a victory's gold, a few crystals from the gem-trader, a spare chest from the smith. After the first land an ad may play between battles, now and then. The Treasurer at the market sells crystals, a thirty-day writ and Quiet March, which ends the ads between battles for good. Nothing sold wins a fight for you.

    
Losing costs nothing. Heroes still learn from a defeat. Change the team and try again.

    
The Endless Gate opens at the camp after chapter 1: waves without end, paid by how long you hold.

    
Your save is kept on this device. Settings → Back up or move save gives you a code that carries it to another one.
